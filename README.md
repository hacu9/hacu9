### Day job

Five years at Level60 Consulting on a room-health platform for corporate clients: it watches
Microsoft Teams Rooms, Cisco and Zoom hardware across customer tenants, runs pre-meeting
checks, raises alerts, and syncs to an ERP, ServiceNow and Power BI.

I joined it two years in; five years later I own its integration and alerting layers and the
Azure platform underneath, from Entra app registrations and multi-tenant consent through Blob
Storage, Logic Apps, Azure SQL, Analysis Services and Monitor data collection rules. When
Microsoft retired the only Graph API that reported Teams Rooms device health, with no
replacement and none planned, I wrote the assessment that determined which device-health
reporting each customer tenant could keep.
